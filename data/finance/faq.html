Acme Robotics Support FAQ

What is the warranty period on the Warehouse Bot X2?

The Warehouse Bot X2 ships with a standard 24-month hardware warranty covering drivetrain, chassis, and onboard compute. The LIDAR Upgrade Kit carries a separate 12-month warranty because the sensor module is sourced from a third-party supplier.

How do I request a field service visit?

Field service visits can be requested through the customer portal under "Support > Schedule Visit". Standard response time is 3 business days in North America and EMEA, and 5 business days in APAC and LATAM due to smaller regional technician teams.

Can I downgrade from the Pro subscription to Standard mid-contract?

Yes, subscription tier changes take effect at the start of the next billing cycle. Downgrading from Pro to Standard disables fleet analytics and predictive maintenance alerts, but does not affect core robot operation.

What happens if a Warehouse Bot S1 is discontinued?

Discontinued hardware models continue to receive software updates and spare parts support for 5 years after the discontinuation date, per Acme's published hardware lifecycle policy.
